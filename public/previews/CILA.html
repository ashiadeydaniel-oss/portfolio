Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our appointments, concierge visits, payment options, and hygiene standards.

Yes, bespoke private house calls and on-location concierge visits are available across selected areas in Accra (including East Legon, Cantonments, Airport Residential, Labone, Ridge, and surrounding neighborhoods). Concierge sessions must be scheduled in advance and include a travel and transport fee based on your specific location.
We accept Mobile Money (MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, and AT Money), direct Ghanaian bank transfers, and cash at the studio. For house calls, Mobile Money is preferred upon completion of the service.
We operate strictly by appointment to ensure privacy, unhurried personal attention, and meticulous sanitation for each client. Simply select your desired service, date, and preferred time using the reservation form below, and it will direct you to WhatsApp where we will confirm your slot.
Client safety is our foremost commitment. All stainless steel and metal instruments undergo rigorous ultrasonic cleaning and medical-grade sterilization between clients. All porous materials—such as nail files, buffers, toe separators, and pedicure tub liners—are strictly single-use and disposed of after every appointment.
Yes, you are welcome to book combination sessions (e.g. Luxury Nails together with a restorative Pedicure or Foot Care treatment). You can select your desired combination in the form below or mention it in your WhatsApp inquiry.
